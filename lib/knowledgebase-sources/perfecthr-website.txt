# Perfect HR Product Overview

## Core Value Proposition
Perfect HR is described as "AI-Driven Modern HR Solution" that consolidates workforce management, payroll, recruitment, and analytics in one intelligent platform.

## Target Audience
Growing businesses with 1-300+ employees. Organizations across various sectors including corporate, education, healthcare, manufacturing, consulting, and financial services.

## Primary Modules & Features

### Employee Management
Comprehensive employee profiles (personal, health, license records). Branch and inter-company transfers. Resignation and exit clearance workflows. Performance appraisals with OKRs and 9-box talent grids. Multi-company and multi-branch support.

### Recruitment & ATS
AI-powered CV screening and candidate ranking. Job positions and application pipelines. Structured interview stages and evaluations. Offer letters and onboarding processes. Talent pools for future opportunities.

### Attendance & Leaves
Biometric and access-device integration. Employee-device mapping with punch logs. Flexible work shifts and punch-time schedules. Configurable leave approval workflows. Sandwich-day rules and notice-period validation. Leave balance carryover with expiry rules.

### Payroll & Accounts
Payslips and batch payroll runs. Flexible salary structures and rules. Worked-day calculations and contribution registers. Automated journal entries to accounting. Employee loan requests with installment schedules. Salary advance request tracking.

### Employee Assets, Applications & Documents
Asset management with allocation and return tracking. Salary, employment, and experience certificates. NOC, ID card, and access card requests. Multi-step approval workflows with recorded reasons. Organized document folders with version control. Document distribution and expiry alerts.

### Task & Project Management
Project stages and Kanban task boards. Task assignment, deadlines, and priorities. Timesheets linked to tasks. Billable time tracking and project profitability. Goal alignment through appraisal OKRs.

### CRM, Sales & Expenses
Lead pipelines with AI-powered lead intelligence. CRM analytics and visualization. Target-versus-achievement tracking. Incentive calculations based on performance. Expense claims, approvals, and reimbursements.

### Analytics & Reporting
Central HR dashboard workspace. Headcount, leave, and attendance insights. AI-powered business intelligence across HR data. Predictive and prescriptive financial analytics. Actionable AI insights embedded in workflows.

## AI-Powered Capabilities
The platform includes seven distinct AI features:
1. AI Recruitment - CV screening, candidate ranking, and consistent shortlisting support.
2. Attrition Prediction - Identifies retention risks through workforce patterns and engagement signals.
3. Performance Intelligence - Continuous AI-powered insights and coaching recommendations.
4. L&D Personalization - Tailored learning paths based on role, skills, and career goals.
5. AI HR Chatbot - 24/7 responses to common HR questions about leaves, policies, and payslips.
6. Workforce Forecasting - Projects headcount, skill gaps, and budget needs.
7. Video Interview Analysis - AI-generated transcripts and signals for speaking clarity, pace, sentiment, and engagement.

## Implementation & Setup
Described as "Up and Running in Hours." Four-step process: sign up, configure workspace, onboard team, manage and analyze. No-code configuration tools available. CSV import capability for employee data.

## Pricing Model
Three subscription tiers based on employee count: Essential (1-120 employees) at 10,000 BDT/month. Professional (121-300 employees) at 18,000 BDT/month. Enterprise (300+ employees) at 20,000 BDT/month. Additional offerings include a 3-day free trial (no credit card required), a 30-day money-back guarantee, volume discounts, pay-per-active-employee model, and monthly billing adjustments as teams grow.

## Security & Compliance
Role-based access control. AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. GDPR compliant. Automated backups and recovery. Audit trails with timestamps and user details. SOC 2 Type II certification mentioned.

## Integration Capabilities
Native integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, Salesforce, Slack, Google Workspace, and Microsoft Teams. API and Zapier support for 50+ additional tools.

## Unique Selling Points
AI built into the core platform rather than as an add-on. Unified platform reduces tool fragmentation. Support for growing businesses from startup to enterprise scale. Industry-specific solutions for corporate, education, healthcare, manufacturing, consulting, and BFSI sectors. No lengthy implementation process compared to traditional on-premise solutions.
